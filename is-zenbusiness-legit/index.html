What does ZenBusiness actually do?

ZenBusiness, founded in 2015 in Austin, Texas, is a legitimate business formation service. They file your Articles of Organization (LLC) or Articles of Incorporation (corporation) with the appropriate Secretary of State, check your business name availability, and provide a dashboard to track your filing status. Their site reports over 950,000 businesses formed and claims an A+ Better Business Bureau rating (which we could not independently verify at time of publication because bbb.org returned a bot-detection challenge).

This is not some fly-by-night operation. They have real employees, real customer support channels (phone, chat, email), and they do file real legal documents with state agencies. The question is not whether ZenBusiness is "legit" in the scam sense -- it is not. The real question is whether their pricing model is a good deal for first-time founders.

How much does ZenBusiness actually cost?

ZenBusiness advertises three LLC formation tiers:

  • Starter ($0 + state fees): Basic Articles of Organization filing, standard processing (7-10 business days), compliance alerts, and a free month of branding tools that auto-renew at $15/mo.
  • Pro ($199/yr): Everything in Starter, plus rush filing (1 business day), EIN filing, an operating agreement template, and one year of Worry-Free Compliance (auto-renews at $199/yr).
  • Premium ($299/yr): Everything in Pro, plus a domain name, business email, document template library, and business website builder.

Those base prices look reasonable -- until you see the add-on stack that most founders actually need:

  • Registered Agent service: $199/yr (recurring)
  • EIN filing: $99 (one-time, but the IRS issues EINs for free)
  • Operating Agreement template: $99 (one-time)
  • Worry-Free Compliance: $199/yr (auto-renews)
  • Advanced Compliance Protection: $299/yr
  • Fast Filing Speed: $79 (one-time)

The pattern is clear: the entry price gets you in the door, then a series of one-time fees and auto-renewing subscriptions push the actual cost well above what the homepage suggests. A founder who buys the Pro plan, adds registered agent service, and keeps Worry-Free Compliance is looking at roughly $497+ in the first year -- before state filing fees.

This is the most common complaint in third-party reviews: not that ZenBusiness takes your money and disappears, but that the checkout flow stacks subscriptions and one-time upsells in ways that surprise first-time founders. NerdWallet flagged the auto-renewal model and the premium pricing for add-ons that competitors offer cheaper or free.

The bottom line

ZenBusiness is a real company that files real LLC paperwork. It is not a scam. But the "$0 LLC" headline is misleading -- most founders end up paying $200-$500+ in the first year once they add the services they actually need, and several of those services renew automatically at $199/yr until you cancel.

If you want straightforward pricing without reading fine print, doola's Starter plan gives you formation, EIN, registered agent, and a US business address for $297/yr with no hidden subscription layers. For first-time founders who want to focus on building their business instead of managing vendor invoices, that transparency is worth more than a "free" starting price.

Quick checklist: what to do before you choose any formation service

  1. File for your EIN yourself first. The IRS issues EINs for free at irs.gov. No formation service needs to charge you $99 for this.
  2. Compare total year-one cost, not the headline price. Add up formation fees, registered agent, compliance, and any recurring subscriptions.
  3. Check the auto-renewal terms. Services like Worry-Free Compliance and bookkeeping tools auto-renew annually. Read the cancellation policy before you opt in.
  4. Decide if you need a registered agent. Every LLC needs one, but you can serve as your own agent in most states if you have a physical address there.
  5. Read recent reviews on multiple platforms. Trustpilot, Reddit, and BBB reviews often surface the checkout experience issues that marketing pages do not mention.
  6. Consider your residency. If you are outside the US, a service like doola that handles international founders is a better starting point than ZenBusiness.

FAQ

Is ZenBusiness a scam?

No. ZenBusiness is a registered business formation service based in Austin, Texas. They file real Articles of Organization with state Secretaries of State and have formed over 950,000 businesses according to their own reporting. Their site claims an A+ BBB rating (unverified at time of publication). The concern is not legitimacy but pricing transparency.

How much does ZenBusiness really cost for an LLC?

The Starter plan is $0 plus state filing fees (typically $50-$500 depending on your state). But most founders add services that push the first-year total to $200-$500+. The Pro plan is $199/yr, Premium is $299/yr, and registered agent service is an additional $199/yr that auto-renews.

Can I get an EIN for free instead of paying ZenBusiness $99?

Yes. The IRS issues EINs at no charge through their online application. It takes about 10 minutes. There is no reason to pay a formation service for this.

Does ZenBusiness work for non-US founders?

ZenBusiness can form an LLC for non-US residents, but it is not their core market and the process is less streamlined than alternatives. If you are based outside the US, doola specializes in international founders and includes a US business address and bank account setup.

What is ZenBusiness Worry-Free Compliance?

A $199/yr auto-renewing service where ZenBusiness files your state annual report and up to two amendments per year. You get the first year free with Pro or Premium plans. It is not required -- you can file annual reports yourself for just the state fee.

Should I use the free ZenBusiness Starter plan?

If you only need the basic Articles of Organization filing and plan to handle everything else yourself (EIN, operating agreement, registered agent), the Starter plan plus state fees is genuinely low-cost. Just be aware of the auto-renewing branding tools ($15/mo after the free month) and the Worry-Free Compliance upsell ($199/yr) that gets presented during checkout.
