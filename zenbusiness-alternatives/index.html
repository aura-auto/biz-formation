Why Look Beyond ZenBusiness?

ZenBusiness has helped launch over 950,000 businesses and holds an A+ BBB rating, so it is a legitimate service. But here is what to watch for:

The $0 plan is a loss leader. The Starter plan covers only the Articles of Organization filing. No operating agreement. No EIN. No registered agent. To get the essentials, you either upgrade to Pro ($199/year) or buy each add-on separately -- the registered agent alone costs $199/year.

Auto-renewal subscriptions add up fast. ZenBusiness products are often set to auto-renew annually. If you forget to cancel, you may find charges you did not expect. Their "Worry-free Compliance" package, which handles annual reports, is priced as a recurring subscription rather than a one-time purchase.

Support can be inconsistent. Customer reviews on Trustpilot and other platforms are mixed. Some founders report slow response times, especially during peak filing seasons.

1. doola -- Best Overall Alternative

When to pick doola: You are a non-US founder needing a hands-off US business setup, or you want formation + bookkeeping + tax compliance bundled into one service without hunting for add-ons.

Avoid if: You are on a tight budget -- at $297/year, doola costs more upfront than the budget options. But you get significantly more included.
Cost: Starter plan: $297/year (LLC or C Corp filing in 1-2 business days, EIN, US business address). Bookkeeping: $300/year. Tax & Compliance: $1,999/year. Business-in-a-Box: $2,999/year.

What sets doola apart is the bundled approach. The Starter plan alone includes formation, an EIN, and a US business address -- three things ZenBusiness charges separately for. doola also integrates with Mercury for business banking, which is useful for non-US founders who need a US bank account. They work with founders worldwide and do not require US citizenship.

For a full breakdown, see doola's pricing page.

2. Northwest Registered Agent -- Best for Privacy

When to pick Northwest: You want a formation service that does not sell your customer data to third parties, or you need strong privacy protections.

Avoid if: You need bookkeeping or tax filing bundled in -- Northwest focuses purely on formation and registered agent services.
Cost: $39 + state fee. Includes free registered agent for the first year, free operating agreement template, and free EIN assistance.

Northwest's core pitch is simple: they do not sell your information. In an industry where many formation services monetize customer data by sharing it with partner companies, that matters. They have been operating since 1998 and have a strong reputation for straightforward, no-nonsense service. Their website is clean, their pricing is transparent, and they are one of the few services that does not bundle formation with a bunch of extras you may not need.

Northwest Registered Agent is also the parent company of their own formation service, so they handle everything in-house.

3. Bizee -- Best Budget Option

When to pick Bizee: You want the cheapest possible LLC formation and plan to handle the rest yourself.

Avoid if: You need an EIN, operating agreement, or ongoing compliance -- these are all paid add-ons, and the total can exceed what other services charge for a bundled package.
Cost: $0 service fee + state fee on the Silver plan. Registered agent free for the first year. EIN: $70 add-on. Operating agreement: included in Gold plan.

Bizee (formerly Incfile) offers a genuinely free basic plan. The catch is that once you start adding services -- EIN filing, operating agreement, compliance filings -- the price climbs. But if you only need the filing handled and want to DIY the rest, Bizee is hard to beat on price.

See Bizee's LLC formation page for current plan details.

4. LegalZoom -- Best for Legal Support

When to pick LegalZoom: You want a well-established brand with access to attorneys and legal document review, not just formation filing.

Avoid if: You want the cheapest option. LegalZoom's plans tend to be pricier than ZenBusiness and competitors, and many features require separate add-on purchases.
Cost: Varies by plan. Basic LLC formation starts around $0 + state fees (limited features), with higher tiers adding operating agreements, EINs, and registered agent service.

LegalZoom has been around since 2001 and has helped form millions of businesses. Their strength is the broader legal ecosystem: attorney consultations, trademark registration, and document review. If you need ongoing legal support beyond formation, LegalZoom is worth a look.

5. Harbor Compliance -- Best for Multi-State Businesses

When to pick Harbor Compliance: You operate in multiple states, need business licensing support, or want a compliance-focused partner rather than a formation-only service.

Avoid if: You are a solo founder forming a single-state LLC. Harbor Compliance is overkill (and overpriced) for simple one-state formations.
Cost: Custom quotes based on your needs. Formation, registered agent, and compliance services are bundled.

Harbor Compliance focuses on ongoing compliance -- business licensing, annual reports, foreign qualification, and multi-state filings. Their compliance management platform can save you from missed filing deadlines and penalties. For straightforward single-state LLC formation, their services are more than most founders need.

FAQ

Is ZenBusiness actually free?

ZenBusiness Starter is $0 in service fees, but you still pay your state filing fee (which ranges from roughly $40 to over $500 depending on the state). The Starter plan does not include an operating agreement, EIN, or registered agent service -- each costs extra. So while the sticker price is $0, the real cost to get a functioning LLC is higher.

What is the cheapest alternative to ZenBusiness?

Bizee offers the lowest upfront cost at $0 + state fees, with a free registered agent for the first year. However, add-ons like EIN filing ($70) can push the total higher. Northwest at $39 + state fees includes more in the base package (registered agent, operating agreement) and may be the better value overall.

Can I switch from ZenBusiness to another service?

Yes. You can transfer your registered agent by filing a change of registered agent form with your state's Secretary of State. Most services, including doola and Northwest, handle this transfer for you as part of onboarding. There is no penalty for switching.

Do I need a registered agent?

Yes. Every US state requires LLCs to have a registered agent -- a person or service with a physical address in your state who can receive legal documents on your behalf. If you formed your LLC through ZenBusiness and paid for their registered agent, you can switch to another provider at any time.

Which alternative is best for non-US founders?

doola is the strongest option for non-US founders. They specialize in helping international entrepreneurs form US LLCs, obtain EINs, and open US business accounts without requiring a US address or citizenship. Their Starter plan ($297/year) bundles everything a non-US founder needs into one price.

How much does an LLC actually cost per year?

Aside from the initial state filing fee, you will pay for a registered agent annually ($100-$200 depending on the provider), any annual report fees your state requires ($0-$300+), and potentially bookkeeping or tax filing if you use those services. ZenBusiness charges $199/year for registered agent service. Northwest and Bizee both offer free registered agent service for the first year.
