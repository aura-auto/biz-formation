LegalZoom is the biggest name in online LLC formation. Five million businesses launched through their platform. That brand recognition is exactly why first-time founders click "Start my LLC" without reading the fine print. The "$0 + state filing fees" headline is technically accurate, but it only covers the bare minimum: Articles of Organization and a business name check. No EIN. No Operating Agreement. No attorney access. And two separate auto-renewal traps waiting to hit your card after 30 days and 6 months.

Here's what LegalZoom actually charges when you build a comparable package.

What does LegalZoom's "$0" plan actually include?

The Basic plan covers exactly two things: filing your Articles of Organization with the state and checking your business name for availability. That's it. You don't get an EIN (which you need to open a bank account and hire employees), no Operating Agreement (which every LLC should have), and zero attorney consultations.

Most first-time founders who pick Basic end up buying those pieces separately anyway, either through LegalZoom or another provider. The $0 sticker price is a loss leader, not a real option for someone starting a business.

What are LegalZoom's auto-renewal traps?

Two charges catch people off guard:

  1. Business Attorney Plan ($49/month): The Pro and Premium packages include "unlimited 30-minute attorney consults on new legal topics for one month." After that 30-day window closes, your card auto-renews at $49/month. LegalZoom's own fine print says "even if you do not use the legal plan benefits or speak with an attorney, you will be responsible for any plan membership fees until you cancel." You must actively cancel before day 30 or get charged.
  2. LZ Books ($9.99/month): Premium packages include 180 days of bookkeeping software. After six months, your card is automatically charged $9.99/month until you cancel. That's $120/year for basic bookkeeping that doola's Pulse plan offers at $300/year (with human bookkeeping) or that you can handle yourself with free tools.

Both subscriptions require cancellation by phone at (888) 310-0151 or through your account dashboard. There's no in-app "one-click cancel" button. LegalZoom's subscription terms explicitly state that they reserve the right to change practices and limits at any time.

Why does LegalZoom charge so much for a registered agent?

Every LLC needs a registered agent. It's required by law. LegalZoom charges roughly $249/year for registered agent service as an add-on. That's a steep markup for what is essentially: receiving mail and forwarding legal documents.

Compare that to:

  • Doola: Registered agent included in the $297/year Starter plan
  • Northwest Registered Agent: $39/year (no formation included, but standalone registered agent is cheap)
  • ZenBusiness: $199/year add-on

If you pick LegalZoom Basic at $0 and then add registered agent service, you're already paying ~$249 for something doola bundles into a complete package at $297.

When to pick LegalZoom anyway

Pick LegalZoom if:

  • You genuinely want attorney access and plan to use the consultations during the first 30 days
  • You need legal document templates (the Pro plan includes 150+ customizable documents)
  • You're forming a more complex entity (S-Corp, C-Corp) where LegalZoom's attorney network adds real value
  • You're willing to set calendar reminders to cancel the auto-renewing subscriptions before they hit

Avoid LegalZoom if:

  • You want transparent, all-in pricing with no surprise charges
  • You're a solo founder who just needs an LLC, EIN, and registered agent without attorney overhead
  • You'd rather put the $49/month attorney fee toward an actual accountant or business lawyer when you actually need one
  • You've been burned by auto-renewal subscriptions before and don't want to deal with it

FAQ

Does LegalZoom really cost $0 to form an LLC?

The Basic plan is $0 plus state filing fees, but it only covers Articles of Organization and a business name check. No EIN, no Operating Agreement, no registered agent, no attorney access. For a functioning LLC, you'll need to add those services, which brings the total well above $0.

How do I cancel LegalZoom's auto-renewing subscriptions?

You can cancel through your account dashboard or by calling (888) 310-0151. The attorney plan must be canceled within 30 days of purchase, and the bookkeeping within 6 months. LegalZoom does not send reminder emails before auto-renewal charges.

Is doola's $297/year really all-inclusive?

Doola's Starter plan includes LLC formation, EIN, registered agent, and a US business address for $297/year. State filing fees are additional (same as every provider). There are no auto-renewing add-on subscriptions built into the plan.

Can I form an LLC without paying LegalZoom anything?

Yes, the Basic plan is $0. But you'll need to get your EIN from the IRS yourself (free but time-consuming), draft your own Operating Agreement, and either serve as your own registered agent or hire one separately. The $0 plan is realistic only if you're comfortable handling all the paperwork yourself.

What happens if I forget to cancel LegalZoom's attorney plan?

Your card will be charged $49/month starting 30 days after purchase. LegalZoom's terms state you are responsible for fees "even if you do not use the legal plan benefits." You'll need to actively cancel to stop the charges.

Is Northwest Registered Agent cheaper than LegalZoom?

Northwest charges ~$39 for registered agent service and has low-cost LLC formation options. They're cheaper than LegalZoom for basic services but don't bundle bookkeeping, tax prep, or attorney access. If you want a simple, no-frills formation, Northwest is worth comparing.
