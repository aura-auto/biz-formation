The short version

TL;DR: Northwest Registered Agent beats LegalZoom for most LLC owners: $39 formation with a free year of registered agent service, versus LegalZoom's $0 formation that charges $249/year for the same coverage.

This comparison is for founders choosing between the two best-known LLC formation services in the US. The decision comes down to one question: do you need ongoing legal services beyond formation, or do you want the lowest total cost with better privacy and support? Northwest wins the second category, LegalZoom wins the first.

How do Northwest and LegalZoom pricing compare in 2026?

LegalZoom looks cheaper on the surface because it advertises $0 plus state fees for basic LLC formation. Northwest charges $39 plus state fees. The advertised rate is not the real cost, because registered agent service is the line item that actually determines your total spend.

Over three years, assuming you need registered agent service (most LLC owners do), Northwest costs about $289 total: $39 formation, free registered agent year one, then $125 per year. LegalZoom runs about $747: $0 formation plus $249 per year for the registered agent. That is a $458 difference for the same core services, per current pricing on both vendor sites.

What does each formation package actually include?

Northwest's package includes preparation and filing of your articles of organization, a free year of registered agent service, an operating agreement template, mail forwarding, same-day filing in most states, and compliance alerts for annual report deadlines. The checkout has no upsells, which is unusual in this industry.

LegalZoom's $0 package includes the articles of organization and a name availability check. Everything else is sold separately: registered agent service at $249/year, an operating agreement at $99, EIN filing at $99, a compliance calendar at $99/year, and a business license report at $99. Decline every add-on and the $0 price holds; accept the common ones and the first-year cost lands in the $400-$600 range.

When to pick: Northwest, if you want formation plus registered agent service bundled at a predictable price with no upsell pressure.
Avoid if: you plan to use attorney consultations, contract reviews, or trademark filings on a regular basis.

Which service has better customer support?

Northwest assigns every customer a dedicated Corporate Guide, a named person who learns your specific LLC situation and answers questions about state requirements and compliance deadlines. Reviews consistently describe these guides as knowledgeable and reachable by phone during business hours.

LegalZoom supports more than 4 million customers, and the volume shows in its support experience. The platform offers phone, live chat, and email, plus attorney consultations for customers on legal plans, but reviews frequently cite long hold times and transfers between departments. The trade-off: LegalZoom is the only one of the two that can connect you to a lawyer for actual legal advice.

How does privacy protection differ?

Northwest has a strict policy against selling customer data, and it uses its own address as the registered agent address on public filings, which keeps your home address off state records. In an industry where several formation services monetize customer lists by selling them to insurance companies, lenders, and marketing firms, this is the clearest differentiator between the two companies.

LegalZoom's privacy policy permits sharing customer data with partners for marketing purposes. If keeping your personal information out of the data economy matters to you, that alone settles the choice.

When should you pick Northwest over LegalZoom?

Choose Northwest if your primary need is LLC formation plus dependable, fairly priced registered agent service. You get the lower three-year cost, a named support contact, industry-leading privacy, and compliance reminders, all without fighting upsell screens.

When should you pick LegalZoom over Northwest?

Choose LegalZoom if you anticipate ongoing legal needs: attorney consultations, document review, trademark filings, or contract templates. Its legal services platform is the reason to pay the higher registered agent fee, and the compliance calendar, while an add-on, is a useful notification layer for multi-state filers.

How do you decide in five minutes?

  1. Estimate whether you will need a lawyer within the next 12 months; if yes, LegalZoom.
  2. Compare the three-year totals from the table above; if cost matters, Northwest.
  3. Check your state's registered agent requirements and annual report fees on the secretary of state website before you commit to either.

FAQ

Is Northwest Registered Agent better than LegalZoom?

For registered agent value and privacy, yes: $125/year versus $249/year, with a free first year and a no-data-selling policy. LegalZoom is better only if you need its attorney consultations and broader legal services.

Why is LegalZoom formation free while Northwest charges $39?

LegalZoom uses $0 formation as an entry point and earns revenue from add-ons and its $249/year registered agent service. Northwest charges $39 upfront but includes a free year of registered agent service, a $125 value, in the price.

Does LegalZoom include a registered agent with formation?

No. LegalZoom's $0 package does not include registered agent service; it costs $249/year separately. Northwest includes a free first year with formation.

Can you switch from LegalZoom to Northwest?

Yes, at any time. Northwest charges $125/year for standalone registered agent service and files the change-of-agent forms with your state on your behalf.

Do these services file your LLC with the state?

Both prepare and submit your articles of organization to the secretary of state. Northwest advertises same-day filing in most states; LegalZoom's processing time depends on the state and the package you buy. State filing fees are separate and paid directly to the state in both cases.
