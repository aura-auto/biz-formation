ZenBusiness Hidden Fees: The Real Cost Behind the $0 Formation

Short answer: ZenBusiness advertises "$0 + state fees" for LLC formation, but the real first-year cost lands between $398 and $676 depending on what you actually need. The free Starter plan auto-renews into a $199/year compliance subscription, registered agent service runs another $199/year if you need it, and essential items like an EIN ($99) and operating agreement ($99) cost extra. If you want transparent all-in pricing without surprise renewals, doola's Starter plan at $297/year includes LLC formation, EIN, and a US business address with no hidden auto-renewals.

The Starter plan is a loss leader designed to funnel you into recurring subscriptions. The catch most first-time founders miss: it includes a "free trial" of Worry-Free Compliance that auto-renews at $199/year without requiring your explicit consent at renewal time. Their website, domain, and email tools trial renews at $149/month according to their FAQ.

The "real year-1 cost" range accounts for whether you need registered agent service (most out-of-state founders do) and whether you grab the operating agreement and EIN through ZenBusiness or handle them yourself. State filing fees are separate and range from about $40 (Kentucky) to $500 (Massachusetts).

Which "Free" Extras Actually Auto-Renew?

ZenBusiness uses a trial-to-subscription model on several products. This is legal and disclosed in their terms, but the UX makes it easy to miss. Here's what renews:

  • Worry-Free Compliance: Free first year on Starter, then $199/year. This handles your annual report filings, which many states require. Miss the renewal and you could lose good standing.
  • Website, Domain, and Email Tools: 30-day trial, then $149/month (or $15/month for basic). The pricing page says $129/year for the website builder, but the FAQ text references monthly rates. Either way, this is not free.
  • Domain Name: $25/year renewal. Standard pricing, but bundled into the "free trial" pitch.
  • Money Pro Bookkeeping: 60-day trial on Premium, then $30/month ($360/year).

ZenBusiness's terms state that subscriptions "automatically renew at the end of each subscription period" and your payment method "will be charged each month or year unless you notify ZenBusiness and terminate the services." Cancellation requires contacting their support page. No self-service cancel button is mentioned in the terms.

What Happens If You Try to Get a Refund?

ZenBusiness offers a 60-day money-back guarantee on formation plans, but it comes with significant conditions:

  • State filing fees are non-refundable once documents are submitted to the government.
  • If any product in a bundled order has been submitted to a government agency, the entire order becomes non-refundable.
  • Registered Agent: Refundable within 30 days only, and only before ZenBusiness is appointed as your agent with the state.
  • Non-subscription products (operating agreement, documents, logo): 7-day refund window after purchase.
  • Annual subscriptions: 30-day refund window, less any state or third-party fees. After 30 days, they cancel but no prorated refund.
  • Monthly subscriptions: No prorated refunds at all.
  • Domains: Non-refundable once registered. Registration typically happens immediately.

The most painful scenario: you sign up for Starter, ZenBusiness files your Articles of Organization (consuming the state filing fee), and you decide within 60 days that you don't want the service. You can cancel the subscription, but you won't get back the state filing fee. If you also activated the compliance trial or any other bundled product that was "initiated," the refund window may not apply at all.

When Should You Pick ZenBusiness Over doola?

ZenBusiness makes sense if:

  • You're forming in your home state and already have a registered agent (so you skip the $199/year fee).
  • You don't need an EIN through them (you can get one free from the IRS directly).
  • You're comfortable managing your own annual compliance filings and won't auto-renew the Worry-Free Compliance trial.
  • You value the Velo AI assistant and want the broader ZenBusiness ecosystem of banking, bookkeeping, and website tools.

ZenBusiness is the wrong choice if:

  • You want predictable pricing with no auto-renewal gotchas.
  • You need a registered agent and don't want to pay $199/year on top of formation.
  • You're a non-US founder forming a US LLC.
  • You want EIN filing included without paying an extra $99.

Other Competitors Worth Considering

Northwest Registered Agent charges around $39 + state fees for LLC formation and includes a year of registered agent service. Their pricing is simpler, though they don't bundle compliance filing or bookkeeping. Good for founders who want a clean, no-frills formation with solid registered agent service.

Bizee (formerly Incfile) offers a free Silver plan with LLC formation and a year of registered agent service included. Their Gold ($199) and Platinum ($299) tiers add operating agreements, EIN filing, and expedited processing. Similar to ZenBusiness in structure but with the registered agent included free for the first year on all plans.

The Bottom Line

ZenBusiness's "$0 formation" is technically accurate but practically misleading. The services you actually need to run a compliant LLC (registered agent, operating agreement, EIN, annual compliance) push the real cost well above $400 in year one, with auto-renewal subscriptions that kick in quietly in year two.

If you want a single price that covers formation, EIN, registered agent, and a US business address without trial-period traps, doola Starter at $297/year is the more honest deal. For non-US founders especially, it's the clear winner.

Quick Recap: What to Watch For

  1. The $0 Starter auto-renews into a $199/year compliance subscription unless you cancel.
  2. Registered agent service costs $199/year and is not included in Starter or Pro.
  3. EIN filing is $99 on top of any plan, even though you can get one free from the IRS.
  4. Operating agreements cost $99 unless you're on Pro or Premium.
  5. The website/email trial renews at $149/month (or $15/month depending on the tier).
  6. Refunds exclude state filing fees and have tight windows (7-60 days depending on the product).
  7. Automatic renewal is the default; cancellation requires contacting support, not a self-service button.

FAQ

Is ZenBusiness really free to form an LLC?

The Starter plan charges $0 for ZenBusiness's service fee, but you still pay state filing fees (typically $40-$500 depending on your state). More importantly, the Starter plan auto-renews into paid subscriptions. The formation itself is free; running your business through ZenBusiness is not.

How much does ZenBusiness cost after the first year?

If you stay on Starter, expect $199/year for Worry-Free Compliance (auto-renewed) plus $199/year for registered agent service if you use it. That's $398/year in recurring fees before you factor in any add-ons like bookkeeping ($30/month) or banking ($5/month).

Can I get a refund from ZenBusiness?

Formation plans have a 60-day money-back guarantee, but state filing fees are excluded. If any part of a bundled order was submitted to the government, the entire order becomes non-refundable. Registered agent refunds are limited to 30 days before appointment. Monthly subscriptions receive no prorated refunds.

Is doola better than ZenBusiness for first-time founders?

For most first-time founders, yes. doola's $297/year Starter plan includes LLC formation, EIN, registered agent, and a US business address with no auto-renewal traps. The processing time is faster (1-2 business days vs. 7-10), and the pricing is flat and predictable. The main advantage ZenBusiness holds is its broader ecosystem of banking, bookkeeping, and website tools if you want everything in one place.

Do I need a registered agent if I form with ZenBusiness?

Yes, every LLC is legally required to have a registered agent in its formation state. If you're forming in your own state and can serve as your own agent, you can skip ZenBusiness's $199/year service. If you're forming remotely or want privacy, you need one. Both doola and Bizee include registered agent service in their base plans.

What happens if I forget to cancel the ZenBusiness compliance trial?

You'll be automatically charged $199 for the annual Worry-Free Compliance service. ZenBusiness's terms state that subscriptions renew automatically and your payment method is charged unless you contact them to cancel. There is no self-service cancellation button mentioned in their terms of service.
