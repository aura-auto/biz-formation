How Much Does a Registered Agent Cost in Wyoming? (2026 Breakdown)

Last updated: 2026-08-23

Wyoming is one of the most popular states for LLC formation thanks to low fees, strong privacy protections, and no state income tax. But the registered agent requirement catches many first-time founders off guard, especially when they realize the agent's address goes on the public record.

You can be your own registered agent for free, but only if you have a physical street address in Wyoming and can be there during regular business hours. If you don't, you'll pay a commercial service anywhere from about $125 to $297 per year.

How much does a registered agent cost in Wyoming?

Can you be your own registered agent in Wyoming?

Yes. Wyoming allows any individual who is at least 18 years old with a physical street address in the state to serve as their own registered agent. It's free, and it works if you live in Wyoming, have a stable address (not a PO box), and are consistently available during business hours.

When to pick self-appointment: You live in Wyoming, work from a fixed location during standard business hours, and don't mind your home address being on public record.

Avoid if: You live outside Wyoming, travel frequently, work from co-working spaces, or value keeping your home address off the public record. Most out-of-state founders forming Wyoming LLCs skip self-appointment for these reasons.

Wyoming law requires the registered agent to be physically present at the listed address during normal business hours to accept service of process and government notices. Miss a delivery, and your LLC could be out of compliance.

Why do Wyoming LLCs almost always use a commercial agent?

When you list a registered agent on your Articles of Organization, that agent's address becomes part of the public record, visible to anyone who searches the Wyoming Secretary of State's database. If you're your own agent, your home address shows up. Commercial agents solve this by putting their office address on the record instead.

The privacy benefit extends beyond just the address. If a lawsuit is filed against your LLC, the service of process goes to the registered agent's address. A commercial agent handles it discreetly, whereas you'd personally receive legal papers at your front door, potentially in front of clients or employees.

What happens if you don't have a registered agent?

Wyoming takes this requirement seriously. If your LLC doesn't maintain an active registered agent, the Secretary of State can administratively dissolve your company. That means your LLC loses its liability protection, and owners become personally exposed to business debts and lawsuits.

Before dissolution, you'll typically receive a notice giving you a window to fix the problem. But missing a lawsuit notification because you didn't have an agent could result in a default judgment against your LLC, with no chance to defend yourself.

The safest move: maintain a registered agent at all times, even during transitions. If your current agent is lapsing, file the change before the gap occurs.

How do you switch registered agents in Wyoming?

Switching is straightforward. File a Change of Registered Agent form with the Wyoming Secretary of State, either online through WyoBIZ or by mail. The new agent must consent to the appointment, and you'll need to list their physical Wyoming address on the form.

There's no state filing fee for changing your registered agent in Wyoming, which makes it easy to switch if you find a better service. Most commercial agents handle the transition paperwork when you sign up.

Wyoming registered agent cost breakdown: the full picture

When people ask about registered agent costs, they're often thinking about more than just the agent fee. Here's the complete cost picture for a Wyoming LLC:

  1. Articles of Organization: $100 one-time state filing fee to create your LLC
  2. Registered agent: $0 if self-appointed, ~$125/yr for standalone commercial service, or $297/yr bundled with formation through doola
  3. Annual report: $60 per year required by the state to keep your LLC in good standing
  4. Operating agreement: $0, you can draft this yourself
  5. EIN number: $0, free from the IRS

Your ongoing annual cost is at minimum $60 (the annual report) plus whatever you pay for a registered agent. That's $60 to about $357 per year depending on your agent choice.

Bottom line: pick the agent that fits your situation

Wyoming's registered agent requirement is non-negotiable, but the cost is flexible. If you're in Wyoming with a physical address, self-appointing saves you the annual fee. If you're forming from out of state or want privacy, a commercial agent is the way to go.

For a bundled option, doola's Starter plan handles formation, registered agent, and EIN in one package at $297 per year. For standalone registered agent service with strong privacy protections, Northwest Registered Agent runs about $125 per year. Either way, don't skip this requirement and don't let your agent lapse.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a registered agent cost in Wyoming?

A registered agent in Wyoming costs $0 if you serve as your own agent and have a physical Wyoming address. Commercial services range from about $125 per year for standalone service to $297 per year for bundled formation packages that include a registered agent, EIN, and business address.

Do I need a registered agent if my Wyoming LLC isn't active yet?

Yes. Wyoming requires a registered agent from the moment you file your Articles of Organization. You cannot form an LLC without designating one. The agent's information goes on your formation documents, so it must be in place before you file.

Can I use a PO box as my registered agent address?

No. Wyoming law requires registered agents to have a physical street address in the state. PO boxes, virtual offices without a physical presence, and mail drops are not acceptable.

What happens if my registered agent resigns?

Your registered agent files a resignation with the Wyoming Secretary of State, and you have a limited time to appoint a new one. If you fail to appoint a replacement before the effective date, your LLC risks administrative dissolution. File a Change of Registered Agent immediately through WyoBIZ or by mail.

Does doola include a registered agent with LLC formation?

Yes. doola's Starter plan at $297 per year includes LLC formation, registered agent service, an EIN, and a US business address. The registered agent is bundled into the plan, not an extra charge.

Does Northwest Registered Agent offer a free first year?

Yes. Northwest includes a free year of registered agent service (valued at $125) when you use their LLC formation service for $39 plus the state filing fee. After the first year, registered agent service renews at approximately $125 per year.
