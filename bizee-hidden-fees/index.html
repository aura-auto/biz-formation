Bizee Hidden Fees: What a $0 LLC Actually Costs

TL;DR: Bizee really does form your LLC for $0 plus the state filing fee, but the extras add up fast: registered agent renews at $149 a year after the free first year, and EIN filing, operating agreement, and compliance add-ons cost extra at checkout. If you want one flat price with no upsell maze, doola Starter at $297/yr covers formation plus EIN and ongoing support in a single package.

Last updated: 2026-09-02

Bizee (formerly Incfile) advertises a "$0 LLC" and that part is real. You pay nothing for the formation service itself on the Basic package; you only pay your state's filing fee. The catch is everything around it. Based on official pricing on bizee.com, here is where the money actually goes and when it makes sense to look elsewhere.

What does Bizee actually charge?

Bizee sells three formation packages. The headline $0 figure applies to the Basic tier. The higher tiers bundle extras that are sold separately at checkout on Basic.

PackageAdvertised priceWhat you get
Bizee Basic$0 + state feeLLC filing, free registered agent for year one
Bizee Standard$199 + state feeEverything in Basic plus EIN filing, operating agreement, banking resolution
doola Starter$297/yrFormation, EIN, US address, ongoing compliance support in one price

Bizee's Premium tier runs $299 plus state fees and adds expedited filing and compliance extras. So the real choice is $0, $199, or $299 before the state takes its cut.

Where are the hidden fees?

None of these are scams. They are standard industry upsells. But if you clicked "$0 LLC" expecting to pay only the state fee, each of these can surprise you.

1. State filing fees are passed straight through

This is the biggest "fee" and it is not Bizee's fault. Every state charges its own LLC filing fee, and Bizee's own site notes it runs roughly $10 to $500 depending on the state. California charges $70 to file plus an $800 annual franchise tax. Texas charges $300. Kentucky charges $40. You pay this no matter which service you use, including doola, so always budget the state fee on top of any package price.

2. Registered agent renews at $149 a year

Bizee includes a free year of registered agent service with a new LLC formation. After that it renews at $149 per year as a standalone service, per bizee.com. That is in line with the industry, but it means your "$0 LLC" costs $149 in year two even if you change nothing. If you only need a registered agent without formation, that same $149/year applies from day one.

3. EIN filing is a paid add-on on Basic

An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is free directly from the IRS and takes minutes to get online. Bizee charges a service fee to file it for you on the Basic package; it is bundled free in the $199 Standard tier. If you are comfortable filling out one IRS form yourself, this is pure savings: skip the add-on and apply free at irs.gov.

4. Operating agreement and extras at checkout

The operating agreement, banking resolution, and business document templates are bundled in Standard and Premium but sold as add-ons on Basic. Individually they are small charges, but stacked together at checkout they can approach the price of just buying Standard outright. Rule of thumb: if you need two or more add-ons, upgrade the package instead of buying them one by one.

5. Compliance and annual report reminders

Bizee offers annual report filing and compliance alerts as paid services. The alerts remind you of deadlines; the filing service charges a fee on top of the state fee. These are optional. You can file most annual reports yourself on your Secretary of State website in a few minutes.

Who should still use Bizee?

When to pick: you are a US-based founder on a tight budget, you only need a plain LLC filing, and you are willing to get your own free EIN from the IRS and handle annual reports yourself. The $0 Basic package plus state fee is genuinely the cheapest way to get an LLC filed. Bizee holds a 4.8-out-of-5 rating on Trustpilot, so the filing service itself is well regarded.
Avoid if: you are a non-US founder who needs an EIN without an SSN, ongoing tax and compliance help, or a single vendor that handles formation plus bookkeeping. That is doola's home turf: Starter at $297/yr includes formation, EIN filing, a US address, and compliance support aimed at international founders.

The verdict: Bizee or doola?

If rock-bottom upfront cost is the goal and you will DIY the extras, Bizee Basic at $0 plus state fee is hard to beat. Just go in knowing year two brings a $149 agent renewal and that every checkout add-on erodes the savings.

FAQ

Is Bizee really free?

The Basic formation service is $0; you still pay your state's LLC filing fee ($10-$500 depending on the state). Add-ons and the year-two $149 registered agent renewal cost extra.

How much is Bizee Premium?

Bizee's Premium package is $299 plus state fees, adding expedited filing and compliance extras on top of everything in Standard ($199 plus state fees).

Can I get an EIN for free instead of paying Bizee?

Yes. The IRS issues EINs free through its online application. Bizee's EIN service is a convenience fee for filing it on your behalf.

Can I cancel Bizee's registered agent service?

Yes. You can switch to another agent or serve as your own where your state allows it. File a change-of-agent form with your state so you do not miss legal mail during the switch.

Is doola better than Bizee?

For the cheapest bare filing, Bizee wins. For a flat all-in price with EIN and ongoing support, especially for non-US founders, doola Starter at $297/yr is the simpler option.
