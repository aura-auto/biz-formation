Doola Hidden Fees: What They Don't Tell You Before You Sign Up

2026-08-21 · by Aura Editorial · doola hidden-fees llc-formation pricing business-formation doola-fees

Last updated: 2026-08-21

Short answer: Doola's LLC formation starts at $297/year on the Starter plan, but that covers filing only. Bookkeeping, tax filing, and compliance each cost extra, pushing the real all-in cost to $597-$2,999/year depending on what you need. For price-sensitive founders, ZenBusiness and Bizee both offer free formation tiers (you pay state fees only), while Northwest charges a flat $39 plus state fee with free registered agent for year one.

What does doola actually cost?

Doola markets itself as an all-in-one back-office solution for entrepreneurs, especially non-US founders who need help forming a US LLC. The pitch is simple: pay a single annual fee and doola handles formation, bookkeeping, taxes, and compliance. But "all-in-one" comes with a price tag that many first-time founders underestimate.

Here is doola's official pricing as of August 2026, pulled directly from doola.com/pricing:

Plan Annual Price What's included
Starter $297/year LLC or C Corp formation, EIN, US business address
Pulse (Bookkeeping) $300/year Automated bookkeeping, expense tracking, bank sync
Tax & Compliance $1,999/year Everything in Starter + federal and state tax filing + 1:1 tax consultation
Business-in-a-Box $2,999/year Everything above + monthly financial statements, synced bookkeeping and taxes, quarterly estimates

Every plan is billed annually. There is no monthly option for the Starter plan.

The fees nobody mentions upfront

The $297 Starter price is straightforward, but it is not the full picture. Here is what costs extra:

  • State filing fees: Not included in any doola plan. These range from $50 (Kentucky) to $500 (Massachusetts) depending on your state. Doola does not absorb this cost.
  • Registered agent service: Doola includes a US business address in the Starter plan, but ongoing registered agent service for annual compliance is part of the higher tiers. If you just want formation, you may need to arrange your own registered agent after year one.
  • Bookkeeping: Separate $300/year add-on. Not included in Starter. If you want doola to handle your books, the cheapest path is Starter ($297) + Pulse ($300) = $597/year before state fees.
  • Tax filing: Only included in the $1,999/year Tax & Compliance plan. If you form an LLC through doola's Starter plan and later need tax help, you are upgrading to a plan that costs nearly 7x the base price.
  • Annual compliance renewals: Doola handles ongoing compliance in the higher tiers, but the Starter plan is formation-only. You will need to track your own annual report deadlines and state renewals unless you upgrade.
  • Currency conversion: For non-US founders paying from outside the US, doola charges in USD. Your bank or payment processor may add conversion fees on top.

How doola compares on price

The real question is not whether doola charges fees -- every formation service does. The question is whether doola's pricing is competitive for what you get. Here is how it stacks up:

The pattern is clear. If you only need formation, Doola's $297 is the most expensive base price on this list. ZenBusiness and Bizee both offer free formation (you pay only the state fee). Northwest charges $39. LegalZoom's Basic tier is also free for formation.

Where doola competes differently is the bundled service model. Instead of paying a la carte for bookkeeping, tax prep, and compliance across multiple vendors, you get one platform. That has real value for non-US founders who do not want to coordinate between a formation service, an accountant, and a registered agent. But you are paying a premium for that convenience.

Is doola worth it?

It depends on who you are:

Doola makes sense if: you are a non-US founder who needs a US LLC, wants a single platform for formation, bookkeeping, and taxes, and values convenience over the lowest price. The $297 Starter plan gets you formed quickly (doola files in 1-2 business days), and the bundled approach saves you from juggling multiple vendors.

Doola is expensive if: you are a US-based founder on a tight budget who just needs to file an LLC. In that case, Bizee ($0 formation + free registered agent year one) or ZenBusiness ($0 formation) are hard to beat. Even Northwest at $39 includes free registered agent service and ongoing compliance support.

The hidden cost trap: Founders who sign up for Doola's $297 Starter plan expecting "all-in-one" service often realize later that bookkeeping ($300), tax filing ($1,999), and compliance are all separate upgrades. The gap between the Starter plan and Tax & Compliance is $1,702 -- a jump that catches people off guard.

What to do instead

If doola's fees are more than you budgeted for, here are three paths:

  1. Budget formation: Use Bizee (free formation + free registered agent year one) or ZenBusiness (free formation + Velo AI guidance). You pay only state fees upfront and can add compliance services later as your business grows.
  2. Privacy-focused: Northwest at $39 + state fee is the go-to for founders who care about data privacy. Northwest does not sell customer data, and they include a free registered agent for the first year.
  3. Still want doola: If the convenience of a single platform matters to you, go with doola's Starter plan at $297/year and handle bookkeeping and taxes separately until you need them. Do not upgrade to Tax & Compliance unless your business actually generates enough revenue to justify $1,999/year in tax prep.

FAQ

Does doola charge hidden fees beyond the listed plan prices?

Doola does not appear to charge secret fees beyond what is listed on their pricing page. However, state filing fees are not included in any plan, and services like bookkeeping and tax filing are separate paid tiers, not part of the $297 Starter plan. The "hidden" cost is the gap between what the marketing implies ("all-in-one") and what the Starter plan actually covers (formation only).

How much does doola cost for a full-service LLC formation with taxes?

The cheapest path to formation + bookkeeping + taxes through doola is the Tax & Compliance plan at $1,999/year, which includes the Starter plan's formation services. If you want everything -- formation, bookkeeping, tax filing, and quarterly estimates -- the Business-in-a-Box plan is $2,999/year. Both are in addition to your state filing fee.

Are doola's state filing fees included in the plan price?

No. State filing fees are separate and vary by state. They range from about $50 to $500. Doola will file your paperwork, but you pay the state fee directly or through doola as a pass-through cost.

Is there a cheaper alternative to doola for LLC formation?

Yes. Bizee offers free LLC formation (you pay only state fees) and includes a free registered agent for the first year. ZenBusiness also offers free formation with its Starter tier. Northwest charges $39 plus state fees and includes a free registered agent and ongoing compliance support.

Can I cancel doola and switch to another service?

Yes, but check your billing cycle first. Doola plans are billed annually. If you cancel mid-year, you may not receive a prorated refund depending on their terms. Your LLC remains valid regardless of which service provider you use -- you would need to transfer your registered agent to your new provider.

Does doola charge for EIN registration?

No. The EIN (Employer Identification Number) is included in the $297 Starter plan. Some competitors, like ZenBusiness, charge $99 for EIN registration as a separate add-on. This is one area where doola's bundled pricing is actually competitive.
