Northwest Registered Agent advertises LLC formation at $39. That number is real, but it is incomplete. It covers your Articles of Organization and one year of registered agent service. It does not cover your EIN, ongoing registered agent fees, annual report filing, or the compliance filings your state actually requires. Here is what you actually pay.

What does the $39 Northwest package actually include?

Northwest's $39 covers two things: preparing and filing your Articles of Organization with your state, and one free year of registered agent service. You also get a free operating agreement template and access to their corporate guide service.

That is it. The $39 is a loss-leader. It gets your foot in the door. Everything else costs extra.

Why does Northwest charge $125/yr for registered agent service after year one?

The free first year of registered agent service is the hook. It is standard in the industry, and most formation services do the same thing. But after year one, Northwest charges $125 per year for the same registered agent you already had. If you forget to cancel or simply assume it stays free, you get billed automatically.

This is the single biggest surprise cost. A lot of first-time founders do not realize registered agent service is a recurring expense, not a one-time purchase. When you factor in the $125 renewal, the $50 EIN, and the $9 BOI filing, your first-year cost is roughly $214 plus your state filing fee, not $39.

Is the EIN fee worth paying Northwest for?

You can get an EIN directly from the IRS for free at irs.gov. It takes about 10 minutes online. Northwest charges $50 to file it on your behalf. Unless you genuinely do not want to deal with the IRS website, skip this one.

What do you get with doola instead?

doola's Starter plan runs $297 per year and bundles LLC formation, EIN application, registered agent service, and a US business address into one price. No line-item add-ons. No surprise renewal fees. You pay $297, you get everything a new LLC needs to operate.

For first-time founders, especially non-US residents, the bundled approach matters. You are not tracking four separate invoices from four different vendors. The US business address is included, which Northwest charges extra for ($20-29/month depending on the service).

When to pick doola: You want one price, no surprises, and formation plus EIN plus registered agent plus a US address in a single package. Especially strong for non-US founders.

Avoid doola if: You are on a very tight budget and only need bare-minimum formation. ZenBusiness starts at $0 plus state fees.

What about ZenBusiness as a budget option?

ZenBusiness offers LLC formation starting at $0 plus state fees. Their registered agent runs $199 per year, EIN is $99, and operating agreement is $99. The formation itself is free, but the add-ons add up quickly once you need more than the basics.

When to pick ZenBusiness: You want the cheapest possible formation filing and plan to handle registered agent and EIN yourself (or already have a CPA handling EIN).

Avoid ZenBusiness if: You want an all-in-one solution. Their add-on pricing can end up costing more than doola's bundled plan once you start stacking services.

How do the refund policies compare?

Northwest does not offer refunds on their formation fees once the filing process has started with your state. You can cancel registered agent service, but you will not get prorated refunds for the current period.

doola offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on their plans. If you decide within 30 days that the service is not for you, you can get a full refund. ZenBusiness offers a similar 30-day guarantee on their formation packages.

This matters. First-time founders often change their minds within the first month. A 30-day window gives you breathing room that Northwest does not provide.

What does the BBB say about Northwest?

Northwest Registered Agent is not BBB-accredited. They have an A+ rating with the BBB, but they have not gone through the accreditation process. For some founders, BBB accreditation is a signal of accountability. For others, it does not matter. The distinction is worth noting if vendor credibility matters to you.

doola maintains a strong Trustpilot profile with thousands of reviews from international founders. ZenBusiness has an A+ BBB rating and is accredited.

When should you pick Northwest anyway?

If you only need registered agent service and want to file your LLC yourself, Northwest is solid. Their registered agent reputation is genuinely strong, and the $125/year rate is competitive for standalone agent service. The corporate guide support is also a real differentiator.

Cost: $125/year for registered agent only. No formation bundled.
Avoid if: You want a formation package and think $39 covers everything. It does not.

The real cost of forming an LLC in your state

Whatever formation service you choose, you still pay state filing fees on top. These vary widely. Texas charges $300. California charges $70 plus an $800 annual franchise tax. New York charges $200. Florida charges $125. These are unavoidable regardless of which service you use.

Recap: What to check before you buy

  1. What is the Year 2 and Year 3 total cost, not just the Year 1 promotional price?
  2. Is the EIN included or charged separately? Can you get it free from the IRS?
  3. Is registered agent service included beyond year one, or does it become a separate line item?
  4. Is there a refund window if you change your mind?
  5. Does the plan include a US business address, or is that an extra $20-29/month?
  6. Are compliance filings (annual reports, BOI) included or charged per filing?

FAQ

Is Northwest Registered Agent a scam?

No. Northwest is a legitimate company with over 20 years in business. The issue is not whether they deliver what they promise, but whether the $39 headline matches what you actually pay. Once you add up the EIN, ongoing registered agent fees, and compliance filings, the real cost is two to three times the advertised price.

Can I get a refund from Northwest if I change my mind?

Northwest does not offer refunds once the filing process has started with your state. You can cancel registered agent service, but you will not receive a prorated refund for the current billing period. Compare this to doola and ZenBusiness, which both offer 30-day money-back guarantees.

Is the EIN really free from the IRS?

Yes. The IRS provides EINs at no charge through their online application at irs.gov. The process takes about 10 minutes. Northwest charges $50 to file this for you, which is a convenience fee, not a required cost.

Which service is best for non-US residents forming a US LLC?

doola is the strongest option for non-US founders. Their Starter plan includes formation, EIN, registered agent, and a US business address in one price. You do not need to be a US citizen or resident to use their service. ZenBusiness and Northwest can also work, but you will need to assemble the pieces yourself and may end up paying more in add-on fees.

What happens if I forget to renew my registered agent with Northwest?

Your LLC could lose its good standing with the state. Without a registered agent, the state cannot serve legal documents on your business, which can result in default judgments or administrative dissolution. Northwest sends renewal reminders, but the responsibility is ultimately yours.

Should I just use ZenBusiness to save money?

ZenBusiness starts at $0 plus state fees for formation, which is the cheapest entry point. But once you add registered agent service ($199/yr), EIN ($99), and operating agreement ($99), your first-year total can exceed doola's all-in $297 price. Run the numbers for your specific situation before deciding.
