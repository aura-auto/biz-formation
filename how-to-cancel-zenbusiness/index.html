Here is exactly how to cancel each ZenBusiness service, what happens to your LLC, and a straightforward alternative if you are done with the platform.

How to Cancel ZenBusiness Services

ZenBusiness runs each service as a separate subscription. You have to cancel them individually. The process is different depending on the product and your state.

Self-Service Cancellation (DNR States Only)

Only customers in California, Colorado, Delaware, Hawaii, Illinois, New York, Tennessee, and Washington DC can cancel through the dashboard. Here are the steps:

  1. Log in at zenbusiness.com
  2. Click your profile icon in the top right
  3. Select Account Settings
  4. Click the Subscriptions tab
  5. Find the service you want to cancel and click Cancel
  6. Follow the confirmation prompts

Your access continues through the end of the current billing period. No prorated refund after 30 days.

Canceling by Phone or Chat (All Other States)

If you are not in one of those eight states, or if you need to cancel registered agent, banking, or a formation plan, you must contact ZenBusiness support:

  • Phone: 1-844-493-6249 (Monday-Friday 8am-8pm CT, Sunday 9am-6pm CT)
  • Live chat: Available on the ZenBusiness contact page

Support will ask which services you want to cancel and may try to retain you with a discount offer. You are not required to accept.

Canceling Each Service -- What to Know

Registered Agent ($199/year renewal)

This is the most important one to get right. Your LLC is legally required to have an active registered agent at all times. If you cancel without a replacement in place, your LLC can lose good standing and face administrative dissolution.

The correct order of operations:

  1. Sign up with your replacement registered agent first
  2. File the change-of-agent form with your state Secretary of State
  3. Wait for the state to confirm the change (one to four weeks in most states)
  4. Then call ZenBusiness at 1-844-493-6249 to cancel

Do not reverse those steps. There are no prorated refunds, and a coverage gap during the switch can cost you your good standing. ZenBusiness charges $99 for the first year of registered agent service, then $199/year at every renewal with no discount for staying.

Worry-Free Compliance ($199/year)

This service tracks and files your annual reports. Before canceling, note your next annual report due date in your compliance calendar. Once you cancel, you are immediately responsible for tracking and filing those yourself. A missed annual report can trigger penalties and administrative dissolution.

Self-service cancellation is available in DNR states. If you select "Price" as your cancellation reason, ZenBusiness may offer you a discount to stay.

Money Pro, Domain, and Banking

Before you cancel Money Pro, export your transaction history, expense reports, and invoices -- data is deleted 30 days after your billing period ends. For domain/website/email, disable auto-renew first to avoid surprise charges, then download your content and export email contacts. For ZenBusiness Banking, transfer all funds to $0 balance before requesting account closure.

The formation plan itself is a one-time service. If you have Premium ($349/year) with bundled registered agent and compliance, those are separate subscriptions. Contact support to clarify which parts of your plan are still active.

What Happens to Your LLC After You Cancel

Canceling ZenBusiness does not dissolve your LLC. Those are separate actions. Your LLC continues to exist as a legal entity registered with your state. Here is what you keep and what you lose:

  • Your LLC stays active as long as you maintain your state filings and registered agent
  • Your EIN is yours forever -- the IRS assigned it to your business, and ZenBusiness cannot take it back
  • Your operating agreement remains valid (you downloaded it, right?)
  • You lose ZenBusiness compliance tracking, registered agent coverage, and any bundled services
  • Your data: Money Pro data is deleted 30 days after your billing period ends. Export everything before you cancel.

The key risk is the registered agent gap. If you cancel registered agent without a replacement confirmed with the state, legal notices (lawsuits, government correspondence) may not reach you, and your LLC can face administrative dissolution.

Cancellation Checklist

Before you hit cancel, run through this list: export your data (Money Pro records, email contacts, website content, financial documents), note your annual report dates, sign up with a replacement registered agent, file the change-of-agent form with your Secretary of State, transfer banking funds, disable domain auto-renew, and check your credit card statement afterward to confirm the cancellation took effect.

Refund Policy

ZenBusiness offers a 60-day money-back guarantee on formation services (minus state fees once filed). Registered agent and annual subscriptions get a full refund within 30 days of purchase, but no prorated refunds after that window. If your renewal already processed and you want your money back, call within 30 days.

FAQ

Can I cancel ZenBusiness registered agent online?

No. Registered agent service cannot be self-canceled in any state, including the eight DNR states where other ZenBusiness services offer dashboard cancellation. You must call 1-844-493-6249, and support will only process the cancellation after confirming you have a replacement registered agent in place with the state.

What happens if I just stop paying ZenBusiness?

For recurring services like registered agent and Worry-Free Compliance, stopping payment can trigger late fees and your services will lapse. If your registered agent lapses, your LLC can lose good standing and face administrative dissolution. This is not a clean exit -- cancel properly through the steps above.

Will canceling ZenBusiness dissolve my LLC?

No. Canceling ZenBusiness services does not dissolve your LLC. Your LLC remains a separate legal entity registered with your state. However, if you cancel registered agent without a replacement and miss state filings, the state can administratively dissolve your LLC.

How do I get a refund from ZenBusiness?

Formation services have a 60-day money-back guarantee (minus state fees once filed). Registered agent and annual subscriptions are refundable within 30 days of purchase. Monthly services have no prorated refunds. Contact support at 1-844-493-6249 to request a refund within the eligible window.

Is doola a good alternative to ZenBusiness?

For most small LLC owners, yes. doola Starter at $297/year bundles formation, EIN, registered agent, operating agreement, and compliance alerts -- services ZenBusiness charges for separately. doola also works for international founders who do not have a US SSN. Check doola's pricing page for current details.

How long does it take to cancel ZenBusiness?

Self-service cancellation (in DNR states) is instant through the dashboard. Phone or chat cancellation typically takes one business day to process. Registered agent cancellation takes one to four weeks because you must file a change-of-agent form with the state and wait for confirmation before ZenBusiness will release the service.
